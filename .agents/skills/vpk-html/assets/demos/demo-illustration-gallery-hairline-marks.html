Hairline: Marks

Recreations of hairline's isometric figures, rebuilt by the iso kit from JSON briefs: the marks shelf. Each assembles on entry; hover or select a part to lift it.

The Vercel mark, not yet seated

This is an isometric tribute to the Vercel mark from hairline's "Vercel" figure (Marks shelf). The triangle hangs over the empty slot on its pad, the "no deployments yet" state, and hovering lowers it until the orange base seats.

Mastra: the lone agent

After hairline's Marks shelf. Dimetric tribute to the Mastra mark: an M of three rounded posts, bridged at the top into two humps, on a rounded slab. A lone accented agent waits apart by a dashed neck. Hover to draw it toward the M.

Notion: no pages inside

After hairline's Marks shelf. Dimetric tribute to the Notion mark: a closed box with a centred N in relief on its front and an accented lid resting on top. Hover lifts the lid to show a dashed page outline on the empty top.
