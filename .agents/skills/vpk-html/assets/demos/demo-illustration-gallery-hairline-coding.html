Hairline: Coding

Recreations of hairline's isometric figures, rebuilt by the iso kit from JSON briefs: the coding shelf. Each assembles on entry; hover or select a part to lift it.

Anatomy of a terminal

After hairline's Coding shelf. Isometric terminal window standing upright: window controls, rows of history on its face, the selected line raised out of the scrollback, and the prompt chevron and block cursor at the bottom. The selected line is accented.

Server cabinet, blades out

After hairline's Coding shelf. Dimetric server cabinet on a plinth: a side panel and a thin top cap frame a rack of ten slim blades, each with a vent strip on its face. One blade sits half out and is accented; its neighbours are out less, the far ones flush.

A branch forks and merges back

After hairline's Coding shelf. Isometric commit graph: main along the back, a feature branch that forks off, adds two commits along the front and rejoins main at the accented merge commit, and a new commit about to land. Hover or arrow keys lift a part.

A formatter passing down a file

An iso-kit version of hairline's "Format" figure (Coding shelf): the orange formatter bar lies across a source file, the lines above it squared to one left margin and the lines below still at crooked indents.

Rebuild, change ripples up

A recreation of hairline's "Rebuild" (Coding shelf): a tree of package tiles where the changed parser is accented, and on hover it rises with core and then the app rising after it, each a little less, while untouched packages stay down.

A call stack, opened

After hairline's Coding shelf. Isometric call stack: five rounded frames stacked on a base, main at the bottom and the newest call on top. The two frames above parse lift away to open it; the parse frame, with its local variables, is accented.
