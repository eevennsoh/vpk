Two figure engines, one camera

One document can mix an iso-kit figure and an anatomy hero. They share one camera, one frame loop and one set of grayscale face tones, so the page reads as a single drawing system; the table at the end measures what each engine costs.

Iso kit: the review round trip

Choose the iso kit for figures that sit inside the argument: stacks, trays and assemblies. This one is a short brief; point at a plate, or use the arrow keys, to lift it and name it.

Anatomy hero: the dial indicator

Choose the hero when one machine carries the story and the reader should work it. Slide the carriage; the readout names the block under the plunger and comes from the same model that moves the parts.

How they share the page

One document can carry both engines. They keep one camera, one frame loop, one set of grayscale tones and one frame, and each engine ships its own CSS and runtime block under its own scope.

Measured from the builders' output and this page
Camera45 30 (azimuth, elevation) on both roots as data-vpk-camera; the iso kit and the hero fit their projections to the one shared FIGURE_CAMERA
Frame loopone window.vpkFrameLoop: one requestAnimationFrame chain and one IntersectionObserver for every board; the iso stage and the hero register on it, and off-screen boards never tick
CSS scopesiso kit: [data-vpk-engine="iso-kit"] and the vpk-iso-* classes; anatomy hero: :where([data-vpk-engine="anatomy-hero"]) and the bare iso-* classes; neither selects the other's elements
Face tonestop var(--surface-raised) in light and var(--ill-tone2) in dark, near side var(--ill-tone1) in both, in both engines; an anatomy far side may be one step deeper (var(--ill-tone3) in light)
Lightboxboth interactive roots carry data-vpkh-no-lightbox, so the enhancer never zooms them and never wakes on their motion
Truth modelthe hero's readout comes from present(model(input)): block 1 · reads 18.0 px at rest (0–44 px of travel)
