01 · The brief

Which throw reads as a release?

The same card travels the same arc three times. Only the easing of the throw changes, and the question is which one makes the card look thrown rather than moved.

  1. Question 1
    Does the easing change what the throw means? The film and the cue sheet answer it.
  2. Question 2
    Can the landing hide a weak throw? The lab answers it.
The only variable is the throw's easing.

05 · Engineering

Why one clock

  • Pure functions
    Every pose is a function of the clock, so the film, the lab and the Gantt can never disagree.
  • Held capture
    Frames are held, not recorded, so a slow machine cannot drop or smear a frame.
