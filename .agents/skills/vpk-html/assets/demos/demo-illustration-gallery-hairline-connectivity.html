Hairline: Connectivity

Recreations of hairline's isometric figures, rebuilt by the iso kit from JSON briefs: the connectivity shelf. Each assembles on entry; hover or select a part to lift it.

A patch panel and its cables

After hairline's Connectivity shelf. Isometric rack-mount patch panel with an ear at each end and sixteen ports. Fifteen cables plug out of the front and drop to hang below it; port 16 is empty. One cable is accented. Hover or arrow keys raise one plug or cable.

Dish on a two-axis gimbal

After hairline's Connectivity shelf. Dimetric antenna: a base, an azimuth turntable and a two-arm yoke whose elevation pivots carry a round reflector dish aimed up, two bowl rings on its face and a feed horn rising from its centre. The dish is accented.

A router with its antennas up

An isometric recreation of hairline's Router (Connectivity shelf): a low rounded router body with status lights and side ports, plus four antennas on hinge collars along its back edge. The front antenna is the orange "aimed" focal part, and hovering or using the arrow keys lifts and names any part.

A hub and its eight tiles

After hairline's Connectivity shelf. Dimetric hub: an accented hub with a port sits mid-floor on a flat floor, eight tiles on a three-by-three grid around it, a dashed link from the hub to each side tile. Hover or arrow keys lift one tile.

A message relays hop by hop

After hairline's Connectivity shelf. Dimetric relay tree: a raised hub with four dashed branches; each runs to a relay tile, and three relays fork out to two leaf tiles. The east relay is lit on the way to the accented leaf. Hover or arrow keys lift one tile.

Nodes settle around a hub

After hairline's Connectivity shelf. Isometric network: a large hub tile, accented, at the centre, ringed by eight dashed slots, with eight small node tiles scattered and floating at different heights above them. Hovering settles every node down into its slot around the hub.
